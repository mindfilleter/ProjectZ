# Product Guidelines

## 1. Narrative & Tone

The game's narrative, dialogue, and all in-game text will adopt a **humorous and witty** tone. Interactions should be lighthearted, aiming to entertain the player through clever writing and funny character moments, rather than focusing on epic or serious fantasy tropes.

## 2. Visual Identity

The visual aesthetic will be **Modern Pixel Art**. This style features clean, high-resolution pixel art characterized by:
- A rich and vibrant color palette.
- Smooth, detailed animations.
- Modern lighting and particle effects to enhance the atmosphere.

This approach provides a nostalgic feel while delivering a polished, contemporary visual experience.

## 3. Audio Direction

The music and sound design will follow a unique, custom direction. All music for the game is being composed by **Luis**. The sound design should complement this original score and the modern pixel art aesthetic.
